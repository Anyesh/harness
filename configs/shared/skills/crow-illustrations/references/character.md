# Character: Kaag the crow

The recurring mascot. One crow across every image so a reader recognizes it as a
through-line. Working name Kaag (Nepali for crow), rename freely, it only lives here.

## Role
Kaag is not a mascot, sticker, or corner decoration. It is the worker inside the system
the picture explains. In every shot it performs the core action: the thing the picture
is about is the thing the crow is doing.

## Visual form
- Silhouette: a compact crow, solid black fill, strong readable outline. Solid fill is
  the point because it keeps the bird on-model across shots the way a detailed, feathered
  bird never could.
- Eyes: two small white dots (negative space in the black body). No pupils, no eyebrows,
  no eyelashes.
- Beak: short, straight, triangular. Used to carry, drag, peck, or point.
- Legs: two thin twig legs, slightly wobbly. It stands, perches, hauls, climbs.
- Wings: read as small arm-hands when it needs to hold, push, sort, or stamp. Otherwise
  folded flat against the body.
- Line: the same wobbly hand-drawn pen as the rest of the scene.

## Persona
Deadpan, focused, faintly put-upon. A competent worker doing an absurd job without
comment. Never cheerful, never cute, no open-mouth smile, no sparkle eyes, no sweat drops
or emotion marks. The humor is in the situation, not the face.

## Action library
Pick one per shot and the crow does it literally: carry on its back, drag with the beak,
stack, jam a lever, turn a crank, sort or filter with the wings, guard a gate, stamp,
pull a rope, haul a cart, balance on a beam, peck a hole, plug a leak, hold two ends
together, climb a stack, fish something out, feed something in, sweep, weigh on a scale.

## Compositing rule (the removal test)
Cover the crow with your thumb. If the metaphor still fully works without it, the crow
was decoration and the prompt is wrong. Rewrite so the crow performs the core action:
the picture's meaning must depend on what the crow is doing.

## Prohibitions
- No costume or accessories beyond a single prop the action needs.
- No detailed feathers, no texture, no shading on the body. Stay solid black.
- No big round cute eyes, no anthropomorphic facial expression past dot eyes plus beak.
- No standing in a corner watching a diagram. It works inside the diagram.
- No color on the crow itself. It is always black.
